## The grid

Target `mlx-community/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-4bit` × drafts
{`Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct-4bit`, `Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-4bit`,
`Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct-8bit`} × K ∈ {0 (plain baseline, interleaved), 1…8}
× six workload tags (6 train-split prompts each, 36 prompts) × 200 tokens.
Every arm runs every prompt; arms are interleaved per prompt with a rotating
leader; two warmup generations per arm are discarded. One parquet cell per
arm per invocation under `traces/sweep/<run_id>-<stamp>/`.

default buffer cache grew until the kernel reported pressure); the cache cap
and guard-driven pacing fixed it, and session 2 shows zero pressure rows.
Excluded rows are kept in the parquet with their guard fields — nothing is
deleted, only filtered by `analysis.load.clean`.
